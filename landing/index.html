01The problemWhy raids need rules

A coin call today is a tip trap.

Every day someone in a Telegram group or on X shouts "this coin to 2x". People pile in. The caller bought earlier, sells into the crowd, and the followers are left holding the bag.

  • Who bought first?Nobody can see it.
  • Did the caller sell?You find out after the dump.
  • Who wins a miss?The caller. Always.

RAID keeps the part people love, calling a coin with your crew, and takes out the part that hurts them: one person getting out first.

03The war chestKeyless by design

A chest that signs for itself.

Each raid's money sits in a NEAR smart contract, the war chest. It is not a wallet that a team member controls. The chest asks NEAR's Chain Signatures network to sign its buy, so the signature exists but no single person ever holds the key.

  1. Its own account. The live testnet chest is raid-chest-mwab3vnk.testnet. Every raid is tracked inside it with its own pot, backers and line.
  2. Its own key, derived. mpc_bootstrap derives the chest's key from v1.signer-prod.testnet. Anyone can call it. Nobody can export it.
  3. Its own buy. mpc_sign_buy builds the swap intent inside the contract for the whole pot, then asks the network to sign it.
  4. Its own rules. The chest checks a signed 1Click quote on chain before any swap, allows one swap in flight per token, and refuses sells below the line.

05Fees only from profitTry the numbers

If you don't win, nobody gets paid.

RAID takes nothing from your stake. Fees exist only when the line hits, and only out of the profit.

90%of profit to backers, on top of their full stake back
7.5%to the buyback and burn reserve
2.5%to the caller, or to the crew treasury when a crew calls

06Anti-dump rulesWritten in the contract

Every move a bad caller makes, the chest has an answer for.

ABuy before the crew, then sell into them.
"public countdown still running"The buy can't happen until the crew window closes and a public countdown ends. The time is on chain for all to see.
BSell the moment it pumps a little.
sell floor = entry × lineBefore the retreat deadline the chest only accepts a sell quote at or above the line. Nobody holds the coin alone, so nobody can sell alone.
CFlood the raid with one whale wallet.
"raid is full or you are at the per-backer cap"Each raid has a pot cap and a per-backer cap. Anything over it is refunded, not swallowed.
DRush a call with a 30 second window.
"crew window too short or longer than 24 h"The contract sets a minimum crew window so people actually have time to see the call.
EGet paid even when the call misses.
caller fee = 2.5% × profitNo profit, no fee. A caller only earns when backers earn first.
FSwap at a bad price on purpose.
check_quoteThe chest refuses any swap without a 1Click signed quote it verifies on chain, and only one swap per token can be in flight.

Raids are a team sport.

A crew is a Sputnik DAO on NEAR. Members propose a raid, vote on it, and when the vote passes the DAO itself calls the raid. The caller fee goes to the crew treasury, not one person.

  1. Crew calls. A raid is a DAO proposal that calls call_raid on the chest. Raid #6 was called this way on testnet by Frog Legion.
  2. Raid War. Two crews go head to head for 24 hours. Firepower is the USDC backed into their raids, and hits are the multiples they actually realise.
  3. Feed and crew chat. Every raid has its own thread. Sign in with your NEAR wallet by signing a message, with no gas.
  4. Reels. A short video feed where each reel shows the coin, the live phase of its raid and a Back button.
  5. Telegram. /raid PEPE 2x posts a countdown card that updates as the crew fills. Built and tested, not running live yet.

10Built on NEARWhat each piece does

Four NEAR pieces. Each one does a real job.

NEAR Intentsintents.near · 1Click

Runs the chest's one buy and one sell, checked against a 1Click signed quote. Brings in backs from other chains, and lets MetaMask and Phantom users back with a signed message instead of a NEAR account. Pays out into an Intents account too.

11QuestionsStraight answers

Asked a lot.

What happens if the line never hits?

At the retreat deadline the caller set, the chest sells at market. Backers split what comes back pro rata. No fees are taken and the caller earns nothing. You can lose some or all of your stake.

Can the caller sell before me?

No. The caller doesn't hold the coin, the chest does. The chest sells once, for everyone, at the line or at the retreat deadline.

Who holds the money?

A NEAR smart contract. It signs its buy through Chain Signatures, so there is no private key sitting with a person or a server.

Do I need a NEAR wallet?

No. You can back with MetaMask or Phantom by signing an intent, or send SOL, ETH or USDC from another chain to a deposit address.

Is there a RAID token?

No. There is nothing to farm. 7.5% of profit on winning raids goes to a buyback and burn reserve.

Is this live with real money?

Not yet. Everything runs on NEAR testnet with test USDC. The swap steps wait for mainnet because NEAR Intents and 1Click only run there. The contracts have not been audited.

Which coins can be raided?

Any coin NEAR Intents can route to: tokens on NEAR, Solana and EVM chains like Ethereum, plus others Intents supports.
